Why a learned variance matters when steps are few

The two standard choices, β (upper bound) and β (posterior variance, lower bound), after respacing T = 4000 to K steps. With all 4000 steps they are nearly equal except at the first step; with 10 steps β is far larger near the clean end, so σ² = β leaves noise in the image.
